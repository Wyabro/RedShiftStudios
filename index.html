WHAT I OFFER

SERVICES

Project delivery times vary depending on the scope of the project. The timelines on each package are typical edit windows after footage is captured—rush jobs and larger scopes are quoted separately.

Social & short-form

Fast-turn content for feeds: hooks, Reels, vertical cuts, and simple promos when you need volume without a full commercial workflow.

  • Half-day shoot or edit-only from your footage
  • Up to 3 short deliverables (e.g. 9:16 + 1:1 + 16:9)
  • Basic sound mix and captions on request
Typical post schedule after footage is ready: 3–5 business days.
Starting at $300

Commercial spot

One polished hero video for your website, paid ads, or lobby screen—planned, shot, and edited as a single story.

  • Pre-production call, shot list, and shoot day
  • Professional edit, color, and mix for one master
  • One structured revision round
Typical post schedule after the shoot: 5–7 business days.
Starting at $800

Campaign & long-form

Bigger stories: brand campaigns, events, interviews, music videos, or multi-deliverable packages when one spot is not enough.

  • Multi-day or multi-location filming as needed
  • Color grade, sound design, and multiple exports
  • Cutdowns and format variants quoted to match your deliverable list
Typical post schedule after final filming: 7–10 business days.
Starting at $2,000
